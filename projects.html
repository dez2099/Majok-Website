Featured Project

Project Protozoon

Rethinking the way logistics systems move people, goods, and resources across different environments.

◆

The Challenge

We believe that the current logistics system of the world is flawed in many aspects and requires much-needed improvement.

◇

Our Approach

Project Protozoon explores a different approach to logistics through the development of a system designed to operate across a wide range of environments.

+

SEFC

The result of Project Protozoon is SEFC — a proposed logistics system that relies on carriers, pods, and maglev technology to create a versatile transportation network.

SEFC

The system is envisioned to operate beyond conventional land-based transportation and potentially adapt to different environments, including air, water, land, urban areas, and mountainous regions.
